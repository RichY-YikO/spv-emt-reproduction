Code and reproduction materials accompanying the manuscript "State-resolved multimodal contributions to stratospheric polar vortex predictability". The archive contains Python analysis, plotting and verification code; frozen numerical result caches; derived CSV tables; and 14 publication figures (Figures 1-5 and S1-S9) as vector PDFs and 600 dpi PNG previews. Installation, data requirements, methods and figure-to-script mappings are documented in the archive. The software author and copyright holder is Shuo Yang. Version: v1.0.0.

Figures 1 and S1 use standard Pearson correlations of negated raw eigen-microstate temporal coefficients, centered using the mean of each negated lag-paired sample. The fixed-lag and maximum-absolute-correlation selection envelopes are retained as distinct procedures. Raw ERA5 NetCDF inputs are not included. The archive supports redrawing from the included frozen caches; prior validation checked correlations, figure data, vector PDF resources and exact PNG redraw agreement. A fresh-environment installation and the full original-data calculation have not been repeated for this metadata update.

Source data: Copernicus Climate Change Service (2018), ERA5 hourly data on pressure levels from 1940 to present, https://doi.org/10.24381/cds.bd0915c6.
